About Me

When I was a very little kid, I started my artistic career making two-dimensional drawings with Charlie Brown–style dot eyes. Over the years my work evolved into highly detailed graphite and colored pencil drawings.

I specialize in portraits, historical subjects, classic Hollywood, television, and urban scenes filled with detail. My goal is to capture personality, emotion, and storytelling in every piece.

Original drawings, commissioned artwork, prints, and custom wooden projects are available upon request. Pricing depends on the size and level of detail of each project.
